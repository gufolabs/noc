# Native macOS ARM64 Development and Demonstration

## Scope

Development, demonstration and evolution of the NOC system.

## Requirements

NOC **MUST** provide the ability to develop, demonstrate and evaluate the system on native macOS ARM64 environments.

The project **MUST** consider native macOS ARM64 support as part of its long-term evolution strategy.

The system **MUST** avoid requiring dedicated server infrastructure for basic development, evaluation and demonstration scenarios when equivalent local execution is technically feasible.

## Why?

macOS ARM64 platforms are widely used by software developers, technical leaders and startup teams.

The ability to run, develop and demonstrate a complex system on a local machine significantly reduces the entry barrier, simplifies experimentation and accelerates product evolution.

This vision is based on accumulated engineering experience, understanding of modern development workflows and practical observations of how software systems are evaluated, adopted and evolved.

Providing an effective local development and demonstration environment improves collaboration, enables faster feedback cycles and makes the system more accessible to potential users and contributors.
