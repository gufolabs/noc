# Regulatory Requirements

## Scope

All project activities, artifacts, processes, components and delivered products that may be subject to external regulatory requirements.

This includes:

- software development activities;
- software distribution and delivery;
- operation and maintenance processes;
- data processing and storage;
- security and compliance practices;
- commercial and contractual activities;
- other project areas affected by applicable regulations.

## Requirements

The project **MUST** identify and satisfy applicable regulatory requirements.

Regulatory requirements **MUST** be maintained as FRG requirements when they become applicable.

Regulatory requirements **MAY** be introduced from external sources, including:

- governmental regulations;
- industry standards;
- contractual obligations;
- customer requirements;
- certification requirements.

The project **SHOULD** maintain traceability between regulatory requirements and the project requirements that implement them.

## Why?

Regulatory requirements may affect different areas of the project lifecycle.

Maintaining regulations as part of the requirements graph provides a single traceable source of compliance obligations and allows their impact on the project architecture and implementation to be understood.
