# Golden Rule Standard

## Scope

Human interactions, collaboration practices and community behavior within the NOC project ecosystem.

## Requirements

NOC participants **SHOULD** follow the Golden Rule standard:

> "And as you wish that others would do to you, do so to them."
>
> — Luke 6:31

The Golden Rule **SHOULD** be considered when defining requirements related to:

- community interaction;
- contribution processes;
- communication practices;
- collaboration rules;
- conflict resolution.

Project requirements derived from this standard **MUST** be defined separately and linked to this document.

## Background

The Golden Rule is a widely recognized principle of ethical behavior present in many cultural and philosophical traditions.

Examples include:

- Confucius:
  > "Do not impose on others what you do not wish for yourself."
  >
  > — Analects 15:24

- Islamic tradition:
  > "None of you [truly] believes until he loves for his brother
  > what he loves for himself."
  >
  > — Sahih Muslim 45:71

## Why?

Software projects are created and maintained by people.

A shared principle of respectful interaction provides a foundation for collaboration and helps build a sustainable project community.

The standard does not define specific behavior rules. Instead, it provides a source of principles from which concrete project requirements may be derived.
