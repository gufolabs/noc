# Prohibited Dependency Licenses

## Scope

All external components required for development, building, distribution, deployment and operation of the NOC project.

## Requirements

Dependencies using prohibited licenses **MUST NOT** be used in the NOC project.

The following licenses are prohibited:

### Strong copyleft licenses

- AGPL-3.0
- AGPL-3.0-only
- AGPL-3.0-or-later

### GPL licenses

- GPL-2.0
- GPL-2.0-only
- GPL-2.0-or-later
- GPL-3.0
- GPL-3.0-only
- GPL-3.0-or-later

### Other restrictive licenses

- SSPL-1.0
- Commons Clause
- Business Source License versions that restrict commercial usage
- Custom licenses with unclear or non-standard obligations

Dependencies with licenses equivalent in effect to the prohibited licenses **MUST** also be considered prohibited.

## Why?

Strong copyleft and restrictive licenses may impose obligations incompatible with NOC distribution and derivative product models.

Preventing incompatible dependencies protects the long-term flexibility of the project and its ecosystem.
