# Dependency Health Requirements

## Scope

All external dependencies used by the NOC project.

## Requirements

Dependencies **SHOULD** demonstrate active maintenance.

A dependency **SHOULD** provide:

- identifiable maintainers or authors;
- public project information;
- communication channels or issue tracking;
- evidence of ongoing maintenance activity.

Dependencies with no meaningful maintenance activity **SHOULD** be periodically reviewed.

Unmaintained dependencies **SHOULD** be replaced, isolated or removed when practical.

A dependency considered critical for NOC operation **SHOULD** have an active maintenance status.

## Why?

External dependencies introduce operational and security risks when they become abandoned or unsupported.

Monitoring dependency health helps maintain a reliable, secure and sustainable software ecosystem.
